Exam Overviews

Understanding the core purpose, structure, and authorities behind JEE Main and JEE Advanced.

Step 1 Entrance

JEE Main 2026

National Level entrance exam acting as a gateway for premier institutes and the sole qualifying test for JEE Advanced.

Conducting Body National Testing Agency (NTA)
Papers & Structure Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) & Paper 2 (B.Arch/B.Planning). Consists of MCQs and Numerical Value Questions.
Mode of Exam Computer Based Test (CBT) (Drawing section for B.Arch is offline).
Primary Purpose Admissions to NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, and qualification for JEE Advanced.
Step 2 Elite Level

JEE Advanced 2026

The final stage of the Joint Entrance Examination for top performers aiming for premier Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

Conducting Body Rotated annually among one of the 7 zonal IITs under JAB.
Eligibility Criteria Top ~2,50,000 qualified candidates from JEE Main Paper 1.
Exam Format 2 compulsory papers (Paper 1 & Paper 2) held on the same day. Dynamic pattern (MCQs, Multi-correct, Integer/Numerical).
Primary Purpose Exclusive admission into Bachelor's, Integrated Master's, or Dual Degree programs in 23 IITs.

Eligibility Criteria

Verify requirements before applying.

JEE Main Criteria

  • Qualifying Exam: Passed Class 12th or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
  • Age Limit: No upper age limit for appearing in JEE Main.
  • Admission Requirement: Minimum 75% aggregate marks in Class 12th (65% for SC/ST) or top 20 percentile for NIT/IIIT admissions.

JEE Advanced Criteria

  • JEE Main Rank: Must be in the Top ~2.5 Lakh rank holders in JEE Main (Paper 1).
  • Class 12th Year: Must have appeared for Class 12th exam for the first time in either the current or immediately preceding year.
  • IIT Admission: Candidate should not have been previously admitted to an IIT.

Syllabus Highlights

Core topics categorized by subject for efficient targeted preparation.

Mechanics

Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work Energy Power, Rotational Dynamics, Gravitation, Rigid Body Mechanics.

Electromagnetism

Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, EMI, AC, Electromagnetic Waves.

Modern Physics & Optics

Dual Nature, Atoms & Nuclei, Ray Optics, Wave Optics, Semiconductors & Communication Systems.

Physical Chemistry

Mole Concept, Thermodynamics, Chemical Equilibrium, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics.

Organic Chemistry

GOC, Hydrocarbons, Reaction Mechanisms, Biomolecules, Aldehydes & Ketones.

Inorganic Chemistry

Periodic Table, Chemical Bonding, Coordination Compounds, p-Block & d-Block Elements.

Calculus

Limits & Continuity, Differentiation, Definite Integrals, Differential Equations, Application of Derivatives.

Algebra

Complex Numbers, Quadratic Equations, Matrices & Determinants, Sequences & Series, Permutations.

Coordinate & Vectors

Straight Lines, Circles, Conic Sections (Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola), Vector Algebra, 3D Geometry.

Preparation Strategy & Tips

Essential guidance to maximize performance and maintain momentum.

Time Management

Divide your daily study schedule into focused blocks. Balance new concept learning with problem solving and continuous revision.

Revision Cycles

Create short formula notes and summary maps. Weekly and monthly revisions ensure long-term memory retention of critical formulas.

Mock Tests & Analysis

Take full-length mock tests strictly in time-bound conditions. Spend time analyzing weak areas and avoiding recurring mistakes.
